From harbour to your next stop

Let the ferry be the only part of your journey you need to think about

After a ferry crossing, you may still have a drive ahead to your hotel, villa, or another part of Bali. If you are unfamiliar with the harbour or travelling with luggage, finding the next ride on arrival can add an unnecessary decision to your day.

Plan the road journey around your crossing. Share the harbour, your expected arrival, and where you need to go; then confirm the meeting point and how the pickup will be coordinated. Because ferry schedules can change, it is worth agreeing in advance how delays should be handled.

Whether you arrive at Padangbai, Sanur, Benoa, or Gilimanuk, the aim is simple: make the transition from boat to road clearer, with the important details discussed before you travel.
