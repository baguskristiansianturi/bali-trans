Move between stays with less hassle

Check out of one place without having to figure out the next ride

Bali itineraries often mean moving between areas—perhaps from Ubud to Canggu, from Sanur to Uluwatu, or from one villa to the next. With luggage and a check-in time to think about, organising transport at the last minute can make a travel day feel harder than it needs to be.

A private intercity transfer lets you plan the pickup and destination as one journey. Share the addresses, travel date, passenger count, and luggage so the route and vehicle can be discussed before the day you move.

This service is for getting from one place to another, not for booking a full-day sightseeing tour. If you want to stop along the way, mention it beforehand so the route, timing, and any additional cost can be agreed in advance.
