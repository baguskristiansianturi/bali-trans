Terms & Conditions

Temporary draft. These preliminary terms make the booking form's Terms & Conditions link functional. They must be reviewed and replaced with the final policy before launch.

1. Booking requests

Submitting a booking form sends a request to Bali Trans. A booking is not confirmed until our team has reviewed the details and confirmed availability and arrangements.

2. Travel and flight details

Please provide accurate travel information, including the flight number for airport transfers, so our team can help monitor arrival updates and coordinate pickup. Notify us promptly if your flight or itinerary changes.

3. Fare and additional services

Any fare shown in the form is an estimate. The final fare, route, waiting time, extra stops, and any additional charges must be confirmed by our team before the service.

4. Payment

Payment methods and timing are arranged directly with our team. Online payment is currently unavailable unless Bali Trans confirms otherwise.

5. Changes and cancellations

Please contact Bali Trans as soon as possible to request changes or cancellation. The applicable policy and any charges will be confirmed by our team; the final policy is still to be published.

6. Contact and confirmation

Booking details and confirmation will be communicated through the contact channel used for your request. Please review all details carefully before sending.
