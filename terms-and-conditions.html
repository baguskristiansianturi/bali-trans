Transport booking terms & conditions

These terms explain the request and confirmation process for private transport services shown on this website. Please read them before confirming a trip.

Important: Sending a form or WhatsApp message is a request for a quote, not a confirmed booking. A trip is only confirmed after the team has replied and the details, price and payment arrangements have been agreed.

1. Requests and confirmation

  • Provide accurate travel dates, pickup and destination details, passenger count, luggage and relevant flight or ferry information.
  • Availability, vehicle type, pickup instructions and final pricing must be confirmed by the Bali Trans team.
  • Do not assume a vehicle is reserved until you receive confirmation.

2. Pricing and payment

  • Any price discussed should be checked for the exact route, vehicle, travel date, duration and group size.
  • Ask the team to clarify what is included and any extra costs before accepting a quote.
  • Payment method, timing and any deposit requirement must be agreed directly with the team. This website does not process payment in the inquiry form.

3. Pickup time, delays and waiting

  • Flight, ferry, traffic, weather and road conditions may affect pickup or journey times.
  • Share updated arrival information as soon as possible if your schedule changes.
  • Waiting time, parking, port access and any delay-related charges should be confirmed with the team before travel.

4. Changes and cancellations

Contact the team as early as possible if you need to change the date, time, route, passenger count or vehicle. Whether a change can be accommodated, and any cancellation or rescheduling terms, must be confirmed before the booking is accepted.

5. Service-specific details

  • Airport transfer: provide flight details and accommodation address; agree the meeting point and waiting arrangements.
  • Port transfer: provide the harbour, ferry schedule and destination; ferry schedules may change.
  • Intercity transfer: confirm the full route, planned stops and whether any waiting time is included.
  • Private day tour: confirm duration, itinerary, route limits, overtime and whether tickets, meals, parking or other expenses are included.

6. Passenger and luggage details

Tell the team about children, accessibility needs, special luggage or other requirements before confirmation. Suitability depends on the vehicle and must be agreed in advance.

7. Contact and final agreement

Keep the final WhatsApp or written confirmation for your records. If any detail is unclear, ask the team to clarify it before you travel.
