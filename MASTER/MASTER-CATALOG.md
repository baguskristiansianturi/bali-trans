## How to read statuses

- **EXISTS** — document has been created; check its own status for completeness.
- **DISCUSSION** — subject has been identified and is being explored.
- **NOT STARTED** — document is planned but not yet drafted.
- **WAITING FOR DATA** — drafting depends on information not yet supplied.
- **VALIDATION REQUIRED** — information exists but needs verification before use.
- **FUTURE** — intentionally parked; not an active commitment.
- **RESTRICTED** — internal information; do not publish without review.

## Source-of-truth principles

1. Use Master Data for factual business information and approved reference data.
2. Keep operational rules and customer-facing policies in separate records from descriptive data.
3. Keep editorial/content guidelines separate from facts and rules.
4. A draft, proposal or internal note is not automatically a public promise.
5. Record source, owner, status, last-verified date and next review where applicable.
6. Re-check volatile facts such as prices, opening hours, ticket fees, laws, transport conditions and trends before publication.
7. Never invent missing details. Mark them **NOT PROVIDED**, **VALIDATION REQUIRED** or **WAITING FOR DATA**.
8. Respect visibility: internal/restricted information must not be copied into public pages or articles.
9. Link documents to related records rather than duplicating conflicting facts.
10. Update this catalog and the Work Register when new document needs are discovered.
