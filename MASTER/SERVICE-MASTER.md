## 1. Current Service Category

### 🚘 Private Transport

**Scope:** Private vehicle and driver services for airport, port, intercity and private sightseeing journeys.

The current category contains four service types:

| No. | Service name | Working description | Status |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Airport Transfer | Private pick-up and drop-off to or from an airport | Current category item |
| 2 | Port Transfer | Private pick-up and drop-off to or from a port | Current category item |
| 3 | Intercity Transfer | Private transport between cities or areas | Current category item |
| 4 | Private Bali Tour | Private sightseeing and touring within Bali | Current category item |

These are category-level records only. Individual service pages, vehicle rules, operating areas, inclusions, prices, booking flows and detailed SOPs will be defined in their respective Master records as discovery progresses.

## 2. Shared Strategic Context

Private Transport should reflect the positioning described in [Product Foundation](./PRODUCT-FOUNDATION.md):

- premium personal service rather than lowest-price competition;
- Muslim-friendly support delivered inclusively to guests of every background;
- attentive, professional drivers who contribute to the travel experience;
- clear and honest descriptions of inclusions and service boundaries.

Do not assume every service automatically includes every premium assistance feature. Define the applicable standard for each service before publishing it as a guaranteed inclusion.

## 3. Future Ideas Are Not Active Services

Ideas that have not been approved for implementation are recorded separately in [Future Ideas](./FUTURE-IDEAS.md).

A future idea:
- is not an active service;
- does not automatically require a website page, route, code or design;
- is not a delivery commitment or scheduled task;
- may be promoted into discovery or planning only after explicit discussion and approval.
