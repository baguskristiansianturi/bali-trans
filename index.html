FAQ

Before you book.

Do I have to use BDM transport?

No. Transport is optional. You can use BDM, another service, public transport or your own vehicle.

Can I book only an activity?

Yes. An activity or ticket can be booked independently where available.

Is BDM only for Muslim travelers?

No. BDM is built with Muslim-friendly and family needs in mind while remaining open to travelers who value thoughtful planning.

Will BDM have products and food?

Those areas are part of the planned platform and will be introduced gradually through clearly marked launches and Coming Soon sections.
