FAQ

Before
you book.

A few things travellers usually want to know.

Do I have to book transportation? +

No. Some activities can be booked as ticket-only. You can arrange your own transportation if you prefer.

Can BDM arrange my entire day? +

Yes. Depending on availability, activities, transportation and other arrangements can be combined into one private journey.

Is BDM suitable for Muslim travellers? +

Yes. We can help with prayer-friendly planning, halal dining guidance and private transportation.

How far in advance should I book? +

Advance booking is encouraged, especially for private transportation because driver availability is limited.

Can I book only an activity? +

Yes. If an activity offers ticket-only booking, you can simply purchase the ticket and arrange transportation yourself.
