Have another question? ↗
Do I have to use Bali Trans transport?

No. Transport is optional. You can arrange another provider or travel independently.

Are prices and availability confirmed?

Not for every listing. If details are not confirmed on a page, contact the relevant operator before making plans.

Does the booking flow make a real reservation?

No. The current Lovina flow is a front-end prototype. It does not send a live booking to an operator or process payment.
