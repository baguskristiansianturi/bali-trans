BEFORE YOU DECIDE

A few things
to know.

A straightforward plan starts with clear expectations.

Visit FAQs ↗
Can I confirm a booking on this website?

Not yet. Use the information to explore options, then confirm availability, final price and terms directly with the provider. A trip is confirmed only when the provider gives written confirmation.

Are prices and schedules fixed?

They can vary by date, group size, route and provider. Check the final quote, inclusions and schedule before making a payment.

What should I check before a day trip?

Check the meeting point, departure and return times, transport, entrance fees, what to bring, cancellation terms and any weather or safety restrictions.
