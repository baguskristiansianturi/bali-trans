Good to know

A few things
before you go.

We want the next step to be clear, especially while the platform is being built.

Do I have to use Bali Trans transport?

No. Transport is optional. You can arrange another provider or travel independently.

Can I book only an activity?

That is the intended experience where an activity is available. Check the product page for current booking instructions and provider confirmation requirements.

Are all prices and reservations live?

Not yet. Some pages are prototypes. Where pricing or availability is not confirmed, it should be treated as a request—not a confirmed booking.

Is Bali Trans only for Muslim travelers?

No. The platform welcomes all travelers while including practical Muslim-friendly and family travel considerations where useful.
