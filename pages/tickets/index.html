Tickets & entry

Check the entry details before you set off.

Ticket rules can differ by venue and date. Before making plans, confirm the official ticket source, entry time, validity window, age rules, dress requirements and whether a reservation is required.

Confirm whether transfers, equipment, guided services or separate attractions cost extra.

Changes

What if plans change?

Read the seller’s cancellation, refund and rescheduling terms before paying.
