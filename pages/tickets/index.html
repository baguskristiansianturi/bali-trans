Attraction and activity tickets with clear information.

Tickets & entry

Check the entry details before you set off.

Before buying an attraction or activity ticket, check the date and time, entry conditions, age limits, dress code, accessibility, and whether you need to reserve a slot. Use the venue or an authorised seller for the latest rules.

Validity

When can you enter?

Check the date, time slot, expiry and whether the ticket allows re-entry.

Inclusions

What does the ticket cover?

Confirm whether transfers, equipment, guided services or separate attractions cost extra.

Changes

What if plans change?

Read the seller’s cancellation, refund and rescheduling terms before paying.

No live ticket sales

Check the ticket details before you travel.

Ticket sales and live availability are not available through Bali Trans yet. Confirm the final price, ticket validity, entry instructions and refund rules directly with the venue or authorised seller.
