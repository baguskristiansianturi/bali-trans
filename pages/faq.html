Help & information

Good questions to ask before you go.

Here are the practical details to check when exploring a trip, comparing transport or preparing to make a booking.

Planning your trip

Frequently asked questions

What can I find on Bali Trans?

You can explore Bali travel ideas, tours, activities, destination information, ticket guidance, transport options and practical articles. Each listing should explain the experience and the details to check before deciding.

How do I request a tour or experience?

Choose the listing that fits your plans, then prepare your preferred date, guest count, pickup area and any special requirements. An enquiry should be sent through the official contact channel once it is published. The operator must confirm availability and the final terms before you treat the trip as booked.

Are the prices and schedules final?

Only a price and schedule explicitly confirmed for your request should be treated as final. Prices can depend on group size, pickup area, transport, tickets, add-ons and provider terms. Ask for a written breakdown of inclusions, exclusions, extra fees and cancellation conditions.

What is included in a tour?

Inclusions differ by product. Confirm whether transport, fuel, parking, entrance tickets, activity tickets, meals, guide services, equipment and taxes are included or charged separately. Never assume that a stop mentioned in an itinerary automatically includes its admission ticket.

Can the itinerary be customised?

That depends on the service and operator. Share the places you want to visit, your preferred pace, mobility needs and the time you have available. Allow for traffic, meals, queues, weather and opening hours rather than packing too many stops into one day.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

A submitted form or message is only an enquiry unless the provider explicitly confirms the reservation. Before paying, obtain the confirmed date and time, guest count, meeting point, final price, payment instructions, cancellation policy and a reference or written confirmation.

What if the weather or sea conditions change?

Outdoor, marine and island activities can be affected by weather, waves, visibility and local safety decisions. Confirm the operator’s safety procedure, whether the trip may be changed or cancelled, and what refund or rescheduling rules apply. Follow official local guidance and the operator’s safety instructions.

How do airport and port transfers work?

Confirm the terminal or port, flight or boat number, arrival time, meeting point, passenger count, luggage space, waiting allowance and the process for delays. Make sure the quote states any late-night, waiting or extra-stop charges.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

There is no single rule for every provider or activity. Request the applicable written policy before paying, including cut-off times, weather-related cancellations, no-shows, rescheduling, refunds and any non-refundable tickets or deposits.

How can I contact Bali Trans?

See the Contact page for the official contact information. Business phone and email details must be confirmed before customer enquiries are accepted; do not rely on unverified contact details.
