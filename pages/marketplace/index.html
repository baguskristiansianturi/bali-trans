Travel essentials

Bring what you need; buy the rest thoughtfully.

Pack the essentials you rely on, including any medication, chargers, sun protection and items needed for your planned activities. If you buy food, clothing or personal-care products locally, check ingredients, sizing, instructions and any restrictions that apply to taking items home. Prices and availability vary by shop; this page does not sell products or verify individual sellers.
