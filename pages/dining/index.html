Food and local context

Make room for good food in your plans.

Choose a place that fits the area you are visiting rather than adding a long cross-island drive just for a meal. Check the current menu, opening hours, location, payment options and dietary information directly with the venue. If you have allergies or specific dietary needs, explain them clearly and ask the staff to confirm ingredients and preparation.
