SOUTH BALI · SUNSET & CULTURE

Uluwatu Sunset & Kecak Dance

A late-afternoon journey through South Bali’s coastal scenery, ending with Uluwatu Temple at sunset and the traditional Kecak and Fire Dance. Add nearby stops only if timing, tickets and transport are confirmed.

Suggested durationHalf-day: approx. 7–8h · Full-day: approx. 10h
Travel stylePrivate day trip
Best forCouples, families and culture lovers

What this trip is about

This route centres on Uluwatu Temple’s clifftop ocean views and the Kecak and Fire Dance performance. Depending on your starting point and available time, it may also include a beach or a stop at Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK). A Jimbaran seafood dinner is an optional extension, not automatically included.

Suggested itinerary

This is a planning example, not a confirmed BDM Trans departure. Timing depends on your pickup area, traffic, weather, opening hours and ticket availability.

  1. 14:00 · Pickup (suggested)Meet at your agreed hotel or pickup point. Exact time depends on your location and traffic.
  2. 15:00 · Optional coastal stopChoose one nearby stop such as Padang Padang Beach or Melasti Beach. Entry fees and time at the beach must be confirmed.
  3. 16:30 · Uluwatu TempleWalk the clifftop paths, enjoy the ocean views and allow time for entry and local visitor rules.
  4. Around 18:00 · Kecak & Fire DancePerformance start times, tickets and seating depend on the venue schedule and availability. Reserve and confirm directly before travel.
  5. 19:15 · Optional Jimbaran dinnerIf requested, continue to Jimbaran for seafood dinner. Food cost and return transport must be agreed in advance.
  6. 20:30–22:00 · ReturnReturn timing varies with dinner, traffic and the pickup location.

Alternative: full-day South Bali route

If you want more stops before the sunset, a full-day route can combine the coast, a beach, a cultural park and the temple. The sequence below is a planning example informed by publicly listed Bali tour itineraries; it is not a confirmed BDM Trans schedule.

  1. 10:00 · Hotel pickupBegin from the agreed pickup area; an earlier start may be needed for distant hotels.
  2. 11:00 · Nusa Dua Water BlowCoastal rock formations and waves, subject to sea conditions and safe access.
  3. 12:00 · Tanah Barak CliffScenic limestone road and cliff area; confirm access and local fees.
  4. 13:00 · Pandawa BeachBeach stop and time to enjoy the coast; entry and activity costs need confirmation.
  5. 14:00 · Lunch breakRestaurant and meal are not included unless the quote states so.
  6. 15:00 · Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK)Allow time for the cultural park and confirm entry tickets.
  7. 17:00 · Uluwatu TempleClifftop views and temple visit, with enough time to reach the performance area.
  8. 18:00 · Kecak and Fire DanceShow time and tickets must be reconfirmed for the travel date.
  9. 19:00–20:00 · Return journeyApproximate return window; traffic and hotel location affect arrival time.
Choose the route that fits your day. The shorter sunset route prioritises Uluwatu Temple and Kecak, with Jimbaran dinner as an optional extension. The full-day route adds coastal stops and GWK. Do not combine every stop without confirming timing, ticket costs and traffic.
Temple and performance timing can change. Monkeys live around Uluwatu Temple; keep glasses, hats, food and loose belongings secure, and do not feed or provoke them. Wear clothing suitable for a place of worship and follow local instructions.

Highlights

  • Clifftop views over the Indian Ocean at Uluwatu Temple.
  • Traditional Kecak and Fire Dance inspired by the Ramayana.
  • Option to add a nearby beach stop or Jimbaran dinner if the day’s schedule allows.
  • A route that can be adjusted to the group’s interests after the details are confirmed.

What to confirm before you go

  • Pickup and drop-off location, start time, and total duration.
  • Whether the transport is private or shared, vehicle capacity and luggage space.
  • Entrance tickets, performance tickets, meals, parking and other fees.
  • Language support, accessibility needs, child seats and cancellation terms.

What may be included

  • A private vehicle and driver, if specified in the written quote.
  • Temple and Kecak tickets only when explicitly listed in the confirmed package.
  • Optional dinner only when requested and included in the agreed quote.

Not included unless stated in a written quote

  • Temple entry and show tickets if you choose a transport-only arrangement.
  • Meals, personal spending and optional beach activity fees unless itemised.
  • Licensed tour guide, child seat or special equipment unless agreed in advance.
