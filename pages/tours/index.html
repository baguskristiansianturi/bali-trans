Tours & day trips

Spend less time juggling logistics and more time enjoying the day.

A well-planned tour should fit your interests, travel pace and the people coming with you. Look beyond the attraction list: check how long you will spend on the road, how much time you have at each stop, where the day begins and ends, and which costs are included.

Private day

Travel at your own pace

A private itinerary may offer more flexibility, but confirm the hours, route, waiting time and extra charges with the provider.

Shared trip

Know the group plan

For shared trips, check the meeting point, group size, fixed schedule, stops and what happens if you arrive late.
