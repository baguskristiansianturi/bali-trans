CENTRAL & EAST BALI · CULTURE & HIGHLANDS

Ubud & Kintamani Highland Day

Explore a mix of Balinese culture, green landscapes and views towards Mount Batur. This route works best when you choose a few meaningful stops rather than trying to fit every attraction into one day.

Suggested durationApprox. 8–10 hours (planning estimate)
Travel stylePrivate day trip
Best forFirst-time visitors, families and scenery lovers

What this trip is about

Ubud and Kintamani sit in different parts of the island’s interior, so a realistic plan needs to account for road time. A typical route might combine a cultural stop or rice terrace around Ubud with a highland viewpoint in Kintamani. Exact stops depend on your hotel location, traffic, weather and entry requirements.

Suggested itinerary

This is a planning example, not a confirmed BDM Trans departure. Timing depends on your pickup area, traffic, weather, opening hours and ticket availability.

  1. 08:00 · Pickup (suggested)Start early from your agreed hotel area. The exact departure time should be set after checking the route.
  2. 09:00 · Ubud-area stopChoose a cultural site, craft area or rice terrace. Confirm admission fees and time needed.
  3. 11:00 · Scenic drive to KintamaniAllow for traffic and breaks on the route towards the highlands.
  4. 12:00 · Kintamani viewpointEnjoy the Mount Batur and lake panorama where weather and visibility allow.
  5. 13:00 · Lunch breakChoose a restaurant that suits your group; meal costs are separate unless listed in the quote.
  6. 14:30 · Optional nearby stopAdd a coffee garden, temple or another short stop only if it fits the route and opening hours.
  7. 16:00–18:00 · ReturnReturn time depends on traffic and your hotel area.
Some roads can be winding and travel times vary. Ask about walking distances, steps, accessibility and the exact route before confirming. Attraction names, opening hours and any ticket requirements should be checked for your travel date.

Highlights

  • A combination of cultural experiences and highland scenery.
  • Views towards Mount Batur and Lake Batur when weather permits.
  • Flexible stop selection based on the group’s interests and pace.
  • Time for a meal and breaks rather than an over-packed schedule.

What to confirm before you go

  • Pickup and drop-off location, start time, and total duration.
  • Whether the transport is private or shared, vehicle capacity and luggage space.
  • Entrance tickets, performance tickets, meals, parking and other fees.
  • Language support, accessibility needs, child seats and cancellation terms.

What may be included

  • Private transport and driver if included in the confirmed quote.
  • A planned route with agreed stops and pickup/drop-off details.
  • Attraction tickets and meals only if individually listed as included.

Not included unless stated in a written quote

  • Entrance fees, meals, parking or activity fees not listed in the written quote.
  • Coffee tasting, guided activities or shopping stops unless requested and agreed.
  • A guaranteed clear view of Mount Batur; weather conditions can affect visibility.
Availability and pricing: This suggested itinerary is a starting point for planning. Confirm the operator, departure time, final route, inclusions, total price and availability for your date before paying. Your trip is confirmed only after the operator provides written confirmation.
