Privacy

Privacy notice — draft

This draft identifies the privacy questions Bali Trans must answer before launch. The final wording must match the actual hosting, analytics, forms, account features, data recipients and retention practices. Do not publish it as a final policy until those details are verified.

Draft — not final

What must be documented before launch

  • Which information is collected through forms, account prototypes, cookies, analytics or server logs, and the specific purpose for each item.
  • Which hosting, analytics, communication, payment or travel partners process information, including their locations where relevant.
  • Retention periods, access controls, security measures and how access, correction or deletion requests are handled.
  • The legal identity and contact details of the responsible operator and the process for privacy requests or complaints.

The presence of account, wishlist or booking screens does not prove a production account or booking system is operating. The operator must also verify applicable privacy obligations and review this notice against the deployed implementation before collecting real customer information.
