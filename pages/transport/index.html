Getting around Bali

Transport that fits your day.

The right option depends on where you’re going, who’s travelling with you and how much flexibility you need. Start with the journey, then choose the transport that makes sense.

Your options

Choose how you want to get around.

Bali journeys can involve short local trips, airport transfers or a driver for a longer day out. Availability, prices and service terms vary by provider, so confirm the details before you commit.

Private driver

Useful when you want flexibility across several stops. Before booking, confirm the hours included, route limits, waiting time, parking and entrance fees, overtime charges and the pick-up point.

Airport or port transfer

For arrivals and departures, confirm the terminal or port, flight or boat details, meeting point, luggage capacity, waiting policy and what happens if your arrival is delayed.

Short local trips

For shorter distances, compare convenience, travel time and local conditions. Don’t assume every option is available in every area or at every hour.

Before you confirm

A few details worth checking.

  • Price and inclusions: ask what is included and whether parking, tolls, fuel, tickets or overtime cost extra.
  • Timing and route: agree on the pick-up time, itinerary, waiting time and expected finish.
  • Vehicle and passengers: confirm passenger count, luggage space, child-seat needs and accessibility requirements.
  • Provider and cancellation: check the provider’s identity, contact details, payment method and cancellation terms directly.
  • Safety: use a service appropriate to your journey and follow local rules. Never assume a listing alone verifies safety or licensing.
