The service

A transfer that starts with the right details.

A smooth pickup depends on more than the destination. Share your arrival details and where you need to go, then confirm the vehicle, meeting point, waiting allowance and total fare with the service provider.

Airport pickup

From the terminal

Provide your flight number, arrival date and time, terminal and a reachable contact number. Confirm where the driver will meet you.

Hotel transfer

Between addresses

Share the pickup and drop-off locations, preferred time, passenger count and luggage needs. Check whether stops or waiting time cost extra.

Harbour transfer

For ferry arrivals

Include the harbour, boat operator and arrival time. Allow for schedule changes and confirm the meeting point before travelling.

How to arrange your ride

Four details make the request clearer.

1. Share the journey

Tell the provider your pickup point, destination, date and preferred pickup time. For airport or harbour pickups, include your flight or boat details.

2. Confirm your group

Give the number of passengers and approximate luggage count. Ask about child seats, accessibility needs and the available vehicle size before confirming.

3. Check the total

Ask whether the quote includes fuel, tolls, parking, waiting time, late-night pickup and extra stops. Confirm any additional charges in writing.

4. Agree on the meeting point

Get the driver's contact method, exact pickup location and instructions for delays. Do not assume that a generic terminal or hotel name is enough.

Before you confirm

What to check before the ride.

  • Date and timing: confirm the local date, pickup time and expected journey time.
  • Flight or boat delays: ask how long the provider waits and how to notify them if the schedule changes.
  • Vehicle capacity: confirm passenger seats and luggage space, including child-seat or mobility requirements.
  • Price and extras: request the total fare and list of any excluded fees before paying.
  • Provider details: confirm the service operator and official payment recipient.
  • Changes and cancellation: ask what happens if your arrival is delayed, your plans change or the transfer must be cancelled.
