Places & destinations

Give each area the time it deserves.

Bali's roads, traffic and terrain can make short distances take longer than expected. Choose one area as the focus of your day, group nearby stops, and check the journey time close to departure. Build in time for meals, queues and changes in weather instead of filling every hour.

North Bali

Allow time for the journey

If an experience is the main reason for your trip, plan the travel around its confirmed start time and meeting point.

South Bali

Keep the day realistic

Avoid stacking too many distant stops into one day. A shorter list can leave more time to enjoy each place.

Any area

Check details close to your date

Opening hours, access rules and local conditions may change. Confirm current details with the relevant source.

Plan with the details that matter

Check local details before you set off.

Opening hours, access rules, road conditions and local requirements can change. Confirm details with the attraction or an official local source before setting off; use this guide to shape your route, not as a live status feed.
