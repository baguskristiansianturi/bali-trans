NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

A few details to help you plan with confidence.

What is included in the private tour price?

The quoted service includes the vehicle, driver, fuel, parking and applicable toll fees for the agreed itinerary. Confirm any exclusions such as attraction tickets, meals, overtime or extra stops before confirming.

How many hours can I use the vehicle?

The standard service duration is up to 12 hours per day. Extra hours or routes outside the agreed service zone may incur additional charges.

What is the difference between Regular Full-Day Tour and Long Trip?

Regular Full-Day Tour follows a structured itinerary within the designated service zone. Long Trip is intended for longer routes outside that zone or selected early-morning trips. The applicable service and rate must be confirmed in advance.

Can I book a minivan or bus for one day?

Minivan and bus bookings require a minimum rental period of two days. Airport transfers associated with these bookings are counted according to the applicable rental-day policy.

Are all vehicles from 2023–2026?

Regular and premium cars are generally from model years 2023–2026, subject to availability. Minivans and buses may have different model years. Ask the team to confirm the assigned vehicle.

Is my trip confirmed when I send a WhatsApp enquiry?

No. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a vehicle or confirm a booking. Wait for written confirmation of availability, itinerary, price and service terms.
