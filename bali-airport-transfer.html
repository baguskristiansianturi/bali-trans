AIRPORT TRANSFER FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details and clear answers to help you plan a smooth airport pickup or drop-off in Bali.

Where exactly will I meet my driver at Ngurah Rai Airport (DPS)?

After clearing immigration, customs, and luggage claim, walk out into the main arrival hall exit. Your private driver will be standing in the designated driver greeting area holding a sign with your full name clearly displayed.

What happens if my flight to Bali is delayed?

We actively monitor your flight status using the flight details you provide during booking. If your flight is delayed or rescheduled, your driver will adjust their arrival time accordingly at no additional cost.

Can I book both an airport pickup and a drop-off?

Yes, absolutely. Choose From airport to book your arrival transfer from Ngurah Rai Airport to your hotel, or select To airport to schedule your return ride from your villa back to the airport.

Which vehicle size should I book for my luggage?

Standard MPVs (like Toyota Avanza) accommodate up to 4 passengers with standard luggage. If you have 5 passengers, oversized suitcases, or surfboard bags, we strongly recommend booking a larger SUV (Innova Reborn) or a HiAce Minivan for extra legroom and storage.

Are airport parking fees and tolls included in the price?

Yes. Your fare estimate covers standard airport pickup fees and driver services. The price agreed upon booking is transparent with no hidden costs on arrival.

How early should I reserve my airport transfer?

We recommend reserving your transfer at least 24 to 72 hours in advance to guarantee vehicle availability and smooth driver assignment for your scheduled landing time.
