AIRPORT TRANSFER FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Useful details to help you plan a smoother airport pickup or drop-off in Bali.

How early should I book a Bali airport transfer?

Please choose a travel date at least three days ahead. Your requested vehicle is subject to confirmation.

Can I book an airport pickup and a drop-off?

Yes. Choose From airport for a pickup at Ngurah Rai International Airport, or To airport for a transfer from your hotel, villa or other location to the airport.

Which vehicles can I choose for an airport transfer?

Our fleet display includes regular cars, premium vehicles and HiAce minivans. Select your passenger count and travel date to see matching options. The specific unit must still be confirmed for your date.

Where will my driver meet me?

Your meeting point and flight details are confirmed during booking, so you know where to meet your driver. Pickup arrangements can include a name sign and help with luggage.

Are the prices shown final for every destination?

The prices shown are starting prices for a short route. Your final fare depends on the pickup and drop-off locations, vehicle and trip details.
