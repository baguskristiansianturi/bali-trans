# Homepage copy direction — BDM Trans / Bali Trans

**Status: concept draft for review, not approved public copy.** Prepared 17 October 2026 after the business-first roadmap reset. Use only for the homepage. Do not bulk-copy this language to secondary pages. No live product, price, availability, supplier, support SLA or Muslim-friendly operational promise is implied. Product cards must be limited to fulfilment-verified offers.

## Strategic message

Help families and travellers plan a Bali trip with clearer choices and a straightforward enquiry process. Do not position BDM as a large instant-booking marketplace. The site should show what can genuinely be arranged, explain what still needs confirmation, and let visitors ask for a written quote before any payment.

## Homepage section map

1. **Hero:** who this is for + plan Bali around your interests; primary CTA to explore experiences; secondary CTA to ask about a trip.
2. **Choose a starting point:** activities/tickets, tours/day trips, custom itinerary enquiry. Hide or mark unavailable anything not yet verified.
3. **Featured product:** only a product with a confirmed operator and fulfilment. Until then, use an editorial destination/experience guide, not a faux product listing.
4. **How it works:** share dates/group/interests → BDM checks feasibility → receive a written quote with inclusions and terms → decide whether to proceed. Payment is only discussed after a quote and operating process exist.
5. **Why plan with BDM:** verified history and concrete process details only; no invented reviews, instant confirmations or blanket “halal” guarantees.
6. **Practical guides:** useful information that helps visitors compare options and plan.
7. **FAQ + CTA:** explain what is and is not currently bookable; invite a qualified enquiry without suggesting a reservation has been made.

## Copy and product guardrails

- Do not say “book now”, “instant confirmation”, “verified halal”, “private driver included”, “best price”, “available today”, or promise response times unless BDM can prove and fulfil the claim.
- Do not display fake review counts, ratings, booked counts, sale prices or unconfirmed product prices.
- Avoid generic empty claims such as “premium service” unless followed by verifiable specifics.
- If a product is not ready, offer a practical guide or clearly mark it as unavailable; do not dress a concept up as a live offer.
- Make the primary conversion a trip enquiry, not a pretend checkout. A visitor must understand that submitting an enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.
- Homepage copy must be mapped to actual HTML sections and reviewed at mobile width before considered complete. Locale controls must switch the full page copy consistently, update the document language, and pair each locale with its currency: id-ID/IDR, en-US/USD, en-SG/SGD, ms-MY/MYR.
- Arabic is unresolved; do not create a fifth locale without a decision.
